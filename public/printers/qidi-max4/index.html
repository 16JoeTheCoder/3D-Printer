Features explained

  • Build volume. 390 x 390 x 340 mm, the largest build here.
  • Heated chamber. PTC chamber heating to 65 °C. Hotend 370 °C, bimetal nozzle.
  • Toolhead. CoreXY, 800 mm/s toolhead speed and 30,000 mm/s². Hands-free load-cell levelling.
  • QIDI Box (optional). QIDI Box is supported and drawn as an optional add-on. Colour count not stated.
  • Camera. 1080p camera with AI detection.
  • Air filter. G3 plus H12 HEPA plus carbon.
  • Touchscreen. 5" 800 x 480 touchscreen.
  • Spool holder. Spool holder position is schematic.
  • Footprint and power. 558 x 578 x 612 mm. 40 kg (Combo 46.5 kg). Heaters rated 150 W + 500 W chamber + 700 W bed.
