What it means for the school

A 30 cm enclosed printer with an active heated chamber. Two AI cameras watch for failed prints, which matters when a teacher cannot stand next to it.

Dual AI cameras watch for failed prints.

Trade-off

Users report a hardened nozzle is not fitted natively, so watch nozzle durability. The warranty covers parts only.

Nozzle not fitted natively per users; warranty covers parts only.

Features explained

  • Build volume. 300 x 300 x 300 mm.
  • Heated chamber. 60 °C active chamber. Bed to 110 °C, hotend to 300 °C.
  • Toolhead. Dual-gear extruder, up to 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s². Full-auto levelling.
  • CFS. CFS (Combo): up to 4 units for 16 colours. The CFS lists PLA-CF only.
  • Camera. AI monitor and calibration cameras with tangle detection.
  • Air purifier. Built-in air purifier.
  • Touchscreen. 4" colour touchscreen. USB, Ethernet and Wi-Fi.
  • Footprint and power. 445 x 505 x 850 mm (maker figure). 23.7 kg. 1300 W, 100-240 V.
