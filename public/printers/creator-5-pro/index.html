Four toolheads and HEPA13 air filtering

What it means for the school

Four independent toolheads swap materials with no purge waste, and HEPA13 plus carbon filtering is the strongest air protection in the local tier.

4 toolheads swap materials with no purge waste; HEPA13 + carbon.

Trade-off

Harder to maintain. Printing speed is 300 mm/s; the 600 mm/s figure is travel only.

Harder to maintain.

Features explained

  • Build volume. 256 x 256 x 256 mm.
  • Heated chamber. 65 °C active chamber. Bed to 120 °C, hotend to 320 °C.
  • 4 toolheads. Four independent toolheads (FlashSwap) with hardened-steel nozzles. 300 mm/s print, 600 mm/s travel.
  • Camera. 720p camera for monitoring.
  • HEPA13 filter. HEPA13 plus activated carbon.
  • Touchscreen. 4" 800 x 480 touchscreen.
  • Spool holder. Filament feeds through a tube. Spool positions are schematic.
  • Door sensors. Front-door and top-cover detection.
  • Footprint and power. 520 x 443 x 710 mm including filament tube. 18 kg. 1200 W.
