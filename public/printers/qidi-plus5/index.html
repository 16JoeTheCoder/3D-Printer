What it means for the school

A 32 cm heated-chamber printer with a 370 °C hotend. The QIDI Maker app lets a student pick a model on a phone and send it in one tap.

QIDI Maker app: pick a model on a phone, send in one tap.

Trade-off

Proprietary, non-quick-swap nozzle and a longer heat-up. Must be imported.

Proprietary, non-quick-swap nozzle; longer heat-up.

Features explained

  • Build volume. 320 x 320 x 300 mm.
  • Heated chamber. 65 °C independent chamber heater. Hotend 370 °C, bed to 120 °C.
  • Toolhead. CoreXY, 600 mm/s up to 20,000 mm/s² with 9 mm 1.5GT belts.
  • QIDI Box (optional). QIDI Box compatible, drawn as an optional add-on. Colour count not stated.
  • Camera. 1080p camera with AI detection. Tangle detection needs the QIDI Box.
  • Air filter. G3 plus H12 HEPA plus carbon.
  • Touchscreen. 4" 800 x 480 touchscreen.
  • Spool holder. Spool holder position is schematic.
  • Footprint and power. 500 x 488 x 558 mm. 29 kg net. 150 W + 550 W chamber + 500 W bed rated.
