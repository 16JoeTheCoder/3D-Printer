What it means for the school

About 35 cm of print space, so a whole chassis part prints in one piece. Heated and enclosed inside, with a nozzle built for tough materials.

Largest build volume with a heated chamber in the local tier (42.9 L).

Trade-off

Draws about 2,200 W, so the room's power circuit must be checked first. The shop page says limited stock.

Limited stock; about 2,200 W.

Features explained

  • Build volume. 350 x 350 x 350 mm, the largest build in the local tier.
  • Heated chamber. 65 °C active chamber. Hotend 350 °C with a hardened-steel 0.4 mm nozzle.
  • Toolhead. CoreXY motion up to 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s². LeviQ 3.0 auto-levelling.
  • ACE 2 Pro. ACE 2 Pro (Combo): up to 16 colours. The standard version has no colour system.
  • Camera. 720p AI camera with spaghetti and entanglement detection.
  • Air filter. Activated-carbon air filtration.
  • Touchscreen. 4.3" touchscreen. Wi-Fi 6, Ethernet and the Anycubic app.
  • Discharge chute. Left discharge chute, as listed by the maker.
  • Footprint and power. 25.7 kg. 2200 W at 220 V, so check the room circuit. Outer size is estimated.
